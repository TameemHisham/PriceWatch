FUJIFILM XF 400mm f/4.5 R LM OIS Overview

Offering super-telephoto reach in a lightweight, portable package, the XF 400mm f/4.5 R LM OIS WR Lens from FUJIFILM captures distant subjects like wildlife, sports, and more with ease thanks to powerful autofocus and a bright f/4.5 aperture. With a 600mm full-frame equivalent focal length, this lens achieves massive reach and crisp subject separation in a compact body perfectly suited for all-day handheld use.
Lens Design
Weighing less than 3 lb and measuring only 9.4" in length, the lens design is optimized for convenient handheld use from the sidelines to mountain trails. 5.5 stops of optical image stabilization aid stability while shooting handheld by compensating for camera shake. In situations where extra stability is required, the tripod collar foot is Arca-type compatible for rapid mounting on tripods and monopods.
A combination of four extra-low dispersion, two super extra-low dispersion elements, and Super EBC coating preserves accurate colors, strong contrast, and sharp detail everywhere from sunny fields to snowy mountain peaks. The 21-element, 14-group optical design provides the clarity needed when shooting with high-resolution X-series sensors, while the rounded, 9-blade diaphragm delivers a beautifully soft bokeh that's further enhanced by the super telephoto compression.
Linear Motor Autofocus
Enhanced by the powerful subject detection algorithms in X-series cameras, the Linear Motor autofocus system delivers fast and accurate performance in near silence. Additionally, the lens features built-in focus presets designed to grant you greater control over your autofocus to capture fast-moving subjects.
Additional Details
  • Full compatibility with the separately available 1.4x and 2x teleconverters boosts the reach to 560mm and 800mm, respectively, without a loss in image quality.
  • Weather-resistant construction protects the lens against dust and moisture, enabling worry-free use in inclement conditions when used with a weather-resistant X-series camera body.
